# EatUp 🥚🥑🥛

**Stop throwing food away. Start cooking what you already have.**

We've all been there: the forgotten avocado gone brown on the counter, the cream cheese hiding behind the jam, the eggs you *meant* to use last week. EatUp keeps an eye on your fridge and pantry so you don't have to, and gives you a nudge while there's still time to do something delicious about it.

## How it works

1. **Add what you buy.** Milk, eggs, cream cheese, avocados: pop them in with their expiry date and where they live (fridge, freezer, pantry or counter). EatUp suggests a sensible expiry date for you.
2. **Get a heads-up, not a guilt trip.** When something expires today, tomorrow or the day after, EatUp sends you a friendly notification with an idea:
   > *"Your eggs expire tomorrow 🥚 — how about a veggie omelette tonight?"*
3. **Cook, enjoy, repeat.** Tap the notification to see recipes that use up what's about to go off, so less ends up in the bin and more ends up on your plate.

## Made for the way you eat

Vegan, vegetarian, pescatarian, flexitarian or happily omnivore: EatUp only suggests meals that fit your diet, and never anything with your allergens in it. And because the planet matters too, it gently leans plant-forward. The less meat, the better.

## No app store needed

EatUp is a Progressive Web App: open it in your browser, add it to your home screen, and it works like a regular app, notifications included.

## For developers

EatUp is a TypeScript (React + Vite) PWA with a C# (ASP.NET Core) backend and SQLite. It runs entirely on your own machine at zero cost.
